### EDP (Engineering Design Process)
It's finally step 8, Communicating the results, because I finished with the works of my tetris spin off game and presented it. I had two presentations, one was to the class and I had time to explain basically the whole process while the other presentation was an expo elevator pitch and I only had 1 minute to present my ideas. 

### Takeaways from In-Class Presentations
* Practice is important. One specific way of practicing I should have done was recording myself because one thing I needed to work on was my body movements like swaying and eye contact. I think that happened because I was nervous and if I had recorded myself, not only could I get practice that can make me more confident, but I could also pinpoint when I started to sway or not make eye contact and help me avoid it better.
* Transitioning one part of your presentation to another will make you sound way smoother. WHile i was doing my presentation i only said little things like how did i make my game and i thought it made my presentation move on to showing my game more smoothly because I didn't just show it abruptly

### Takeaways from Expo Elevator Pitch 
* How to articulate your ideas is something important. This was something I had to work on because I only had a minute to show off my presentation and I think I got better by just cutting down information and keeping the most important parts of what I want to show off.
* Keep a balance in showing off and explaining. This was something i didnt think about and I could have done better on. When I was giving my pitch, I was trying to show off my game but i couldnt show it off without being awkward because I would be looking at my game and it was a little hard to talk while moving the blocks. For future presentations I will think about how I can keep a balance of both.

### Skills
##### Communication
It's kind of obvious that one skill I had improved was communication as I was doing presentations. I learned to speak into a crowd because there were many people and giving presentations is scary but it gets less and less scary the more presentations I do. I also got better at pitching ideas and saying a short presentation while still being effective. 
##### Embracing failure
My presentation wasn't perfect and there were many things I could have worked on. Even so I didn't get discouraged and it has actually encouraged me to do better in the future. This project helped me be okay with the fact that i constantly fail and instead of sitting there being all sad about it, I use it as a way to learn and do better next time. This presentation definitely made me feel way more prepared for future presentations.
